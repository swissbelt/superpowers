How Partners Are Selected

The Four-Gate Screening Process

Every subcontractor partner is evaluated against the same four gates before joining the BCS network, so you know exactly what's expected before you apply.

1

Insurance & Licensing

Active, verifiable insurance coverage and the licensing required for your trade and service area.

2

Capacity for Recurring Work

The crew and equipment capacity to reliably take on ongoing, recurring service commitments.

3

Alignment with the BCS Backend Model

Willingness to work within the BCS model, where BCS owns the client relationship, scheduling, and invoicing.

4

Geographic Coverage

Service coverage that fits the areas where BCS has government and private client demand.
